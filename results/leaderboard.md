# Leaderboard

Profiles of AI character systems evaluated with the AI Character Memory Benchmark.

**No systems have been evaluated yet.** This page is intentionally empty until results are submitted that follow the [methodology](methodology.md) and include enough material to be reproduced.

## How the leaderboard works

* Systems are listed **by dimension**, not by a single overall rank. There is no composite score in v0.1.0.
* Each entry states the benchmark version, protocol (A or B), replay mode, scoring method, number of trials and *n* per dimension.
* Protocol B (full-context baseline) entries are listed separately from Protocol A (memory system) entries.
* Entries produced by or for the maintainer are labeled **maintainer-affiliated**.
* Differences smaller than the reported trial-to-trial variation are not treated as rankings.
